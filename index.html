01 / About

Science is not just something to memorize. It is something to understand.

I am Kalel Uthuman, an independent learner based in Uganda, interested in how the physical world works and how technology can be built from first principles. My learning spans physics, electronics, artificial intelligence, Python programming, and cybersecurity.

I value conceptual understanding, careful experimentation, and practical problem-solving. I am developing my skills and documenting projects as I learn.
